    Interactive Sound Lab

    Listen to a light curve

    A light curve records brightness changes over time. Select an astronomical object below to hear its brightness mapped directly to musical sound. You can also press Tab or click any point on the chart to hear its pitch.

    Illustrative brightness over time

    The line stays near a steady brightness, drops briefly in the middle, then returns to its earlier level. This is a synthetic example.

    Use Tab or click any point on the chart to hear its pitch and inspect values.

    Educational model for accessible learning.

    Audio controls

    Higher brightness produces higher pitch. Dips in brightness produce lower pitches.

    Audio starts only when you activate playback.

    Cosmic Ear Challenge

    Listen to a mystery astronomical sound and identify the object based on its rhythm and pitch profile.

    Press "Play mystery sound" to start.

    Accessibility & Design

    Built for Accessible Science

    SAGAN is developed under the International Blind Astronomy Olympiad initiative. Every chart includes text-based descriptions, full keyboard navigation, screen-reader live regions, and data sonification so students of all visual abilities can participate fully in scientific inquiry.
